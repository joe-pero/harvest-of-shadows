# Roadmap

Done this season, and what's next.

1. ✅ **Projection mapping with markers**: now its own tool,
   [projection-fit](https://github.com/joe-pero/projection-fit). Project a marker card, take
   one phone photo, and it fits your video to a door, window or wall. A person confirms the
   fit before anything renders.
1. ✅ **Show power controller**: now its own tool, [show-power](https://github.com/joe-pero/show-power).
1. ✅ **Remote flashing over a Raspberry Pi**: [guide](docs/remote-flashing-over-a-pi.md) and
   [`tools/bridge_flash.sh`](tools/bridge_flash.sh).
1. ✅ **Incident reviews**: [17 root-caused incidents from the season](docs/incident-reviews.md).
1. ✅ **Dashboard write-up**: [the garage display as a control panel](docs/control-dashboard.md).
1. **Next season:** the open items in the incident reviews (Wi-Fi dropouts, the camera that
   went blind for a day, re-sending cues after a dropout).

Ideas or questions? Open an issue.
